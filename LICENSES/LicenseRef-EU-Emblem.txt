European Union emblem
=====================

The European emblem is reproduced in this repository solely to acknowledge
funding, in line with the European Commission's guidelines on the use of the
European emblem by third parties:

  https://commission.europa.eu/select-language?destination=/node/1

Permitted use requires that the reproduction does not create confusion between
the user and the European Union or the Council of Europe, and does not suggest
that the user is endorsed by either. No trade-mark rights are acquired through
this reproduction, and the emblem may not be appropriated or registered as a
trade mark.

The emblem is not covered by this project's Apache-2.0 licence.
